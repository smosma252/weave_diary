# 🧵 WeaveDiary

> *Your scattered digital life, woven into something meaningful.*

WeaveDiary is an AI-powered personal diary that writes itself. It connects to the tools and platforms you already use every day — email, GitHub, Slack, Discord, and your calendar — and weaves them into rich, human diary entries posted automatically. Add voice notes on the go, and WeaveDiary transcribes and layers in your emotions and thoughts alongside your activity. At the end of every week and month, it reflects back a personalised growth report: how you performed, where you improved, and how you're tracking against your own goals.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Auto-generated diary entries** — Daily entries synthesised from your connected platforms, written in a personal, readable narrative format
- **Voice note integration** — Record voice notes anywhere; WeaveDiary transcribes and weaves them into your entries for emotional depth
- **Multi-platform sync** — Connects to Gmail, GitHub, Slack, Discord, Google Calendar, and more
- **Goal tracking** — Set personal and professional goals; WeaveDiary evaluates your activity against them continuously
- **Weekly & monthly reviews** — Intelligent summaries that surface patterns, highlight wins, flag struggles, and chart your improvement over time
- **Conversational debrief** — Ask WeaveDiary questions about your own patterns ("Why do I keep avoiding this task?") and get honest, data-backed answers
- **Private by default** — Your data is yours. WeaveDiary reads to synthesise; it never sells or shares your information

## 📖 How It Works

1. **Connect your platforms** — OAuth-based integrations, set up in minutes
2. **Set your goals** — Tell WeaveDiary what you're working towards personally and professionally
3. **Live your day** — WeaveDiary silently observes your activity across connected platforms
4. **Read your diary** — Each evening, a new entry is waiting: a coherent, human narrative of your day
5. **Drop a voice note** — Add colour and emotion anytime via the mobile app
6. **Review and grow** — Weekly and monthly reports show you who you're becoming

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

WeaveDiary takes privacy seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Integrations use read-only OAuth scopes wherever possible. You can disconnect any integration, export your diary, or delete your account and all associated data at any time.
