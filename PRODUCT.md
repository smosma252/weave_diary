# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, founders, and goal-oriented professionals who accumulate digital activity across many tools
but rarely have a record of what they actually did or how they're growing. They're not disciplined
journalers. They want self-reflection without the discipline cost.

## Product Purpose

WeaveDiary is an AI diary that writes itself. It connects to Gmail, GitHub, Slack, Discord, and Google
Calendar via read-only OAuth, synthesizes the day's activity into a human narrative entry, and layers
in transcribed voice notes for emotional context. Weekly and monthly reviews surface patterns and
progress against user-set goals. The core promise: your scattered digital life, made coherent and yours.

## Brand Personality

Functional, intentional, honest. The product does real work quietly; the interface reflects that.
Warm but not whimsical. Serious without being cold. A tool that respects your attention.

## Anti-references

- Bullet-point journal apps (Things, Obsidian, Bear, Day One with its scrapbook feel)
- Note-taking tools that feel like text editors (Notion, Craft, Roam)
- AI-first dashboard aesthetics — feature grids, gradient hero blobs, "powered by AI" badges
- Productivity SaaS minimalism (white, cold, icon-heavy)

## Design Principles

1. Every element earns its place. No decorative filler; if it's on screen it does a job.
2. Function announces form. The design reveals capability through use, not through features listed.
3. Warmth without whimsy. Serious tool, considered aesthetic — not a productivity toy.
4. Honest over polished. Real language, real data, no marketing softening.
5. Calm by default. The interface never competes for attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA sensible defaults. Reduced-motion consideration for scroll-driven animations.
